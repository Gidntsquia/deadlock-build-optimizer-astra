# Adaptive consensus purchase plan

Before evaluation: retain consensus weights and the existing evaluator. Replace the arbitrary five-purchases-per-phase quota with a bounded, evidence-driven purchase plan. Target the number of shop items purchased in at least 30% of aggregate hero matches, with a minimum of 12 and maximum of 24 purchase events. This is cohort frequency, never reference-player frequency. Select using the existing score; schedule the strongest remaining candidates by aggregate mean purchase time with components before upgrades. Label phases using observed time (through 10 minutes early, through 20 minutes mid, then late), monotonically as the plan progresses. Missing timing uses tier-based fallback times.

Preserve component credits, uniqueness, 12 occupied inventory slots and at most four active items. Tests must assert variable purchase counts, complete ability paths and source isolation. Compare against the unchanged reused 30-match benchmark; do not describe this as independent generalization. No item IDs from that benchmark enter selection.

User steering: prioritize top-level players. The previous 1,000-match gate discarded Infernus's 585 Ascendant+ matches entirely. Lower cohort eligibility to 200 hero matches and 12 item rows; use eligible expert evidence exclusively for item scoring, timing, plan size and pairs. Preserve the 25/25 win prior and preference for items with 20 observations. Fetch expert rows with at least five observations in the exact existing training window. Ability paths prefer expert data, but explicitly use all-skill path evidence if no complete expert path is available, rather than inventing an expert order. No score-weight search or reference-derived selection.
